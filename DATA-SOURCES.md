# Football data sources

Historical country squads and head coaches were extracted from FIFA's published World Cup Qatar 2022 squad list:
https://fdp.fifa.org/assetspublic/ce44/pdf/SquadLists-English.pdf

FIFA announcement:
https://inside.fifa.com/tournaments/mens/worldcup/qatar2022/news/qatar-2022-squads-officially-confirmed

This is a historical published snapshot, not live/current squad or manager data. Names, broad positions and caps were used. Game XIs choose one goalkeeper, four defenders, three midfielders and three forwards prioritising caps within each role. If a category is short, the game fills from other squad members. These are game selections, not official match starting XIs.

The classic groups follow the 2022 field with Nigeria replacing Qatar. The original Qatar record remains in the data file for source completeness but is not in the fantasy tournament. There are 31 real historical opposition nations and the user's all-time Nigeria draft. Ratings and simulated results are original game parameters, not FIFA rankings.

Country/player names are used descriptively. No official competition logo, tournament artwork, trophy image or kit illustration is used. Photos have their own attribution register in ASSETS.md.
